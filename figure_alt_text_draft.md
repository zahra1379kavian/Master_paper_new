# Draft figure alt text for Frontiers submission

Figure 1. Schematic of the hand-squeeze fMRI task and the analysis pipeline, showing reward cue, delay, go cue, grip response, trial-wise GLM beta maps, BOLD signals, reaction time measures, and the optimized vigour-related projection and voxel-weight map.

Figure 2. MNI brain slice overlays showing the spatial distribution of the candidate vigour-related network, with suprathreshold voxels highlighted as red contours across sagittal, coronal, and axial views.

Figure 3. MNI brain slice overlays comparing the vigour-related network with the task-engagement reference map, showing the full optimization network, the reference network, and their small voxelwise overlap.

Figure 4. Boxplot and individual points showing cross-subject spectral distances among ROI-edge connectivity matrices for OFF-OFF, ON-ON, and OFF-ON medication-state comparisons.

Supplementary Figure 1. Ablation-model comparison showing fold-level objective scores and weighted objective-term contributions across optimization settings.

Supplementary Figure 2. Paired run-level comparison of consecutive-trial variability between reaction time and the optimized projection signal.

Supplementary Figure 3. Comparison of selected network voxels with non-selected motor-area voxels, showing low-variability enrichment and a null distribution from size-matched resampling.

Supplementary Figure 4. Heatmap of subject-wise changes in top ROI-to-ROI connectivity edges between ON- and OFF-medication sessions.

Supplementary Figure 5. Subject-level OFF and ON values of normalized absolute consecutive-trial beta differences for selected and control voxel sets.
